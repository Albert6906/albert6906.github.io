🆘 Troubleshooting & FAQ

My download says "unknown publisher" – is that safe?

Yes. Our software is open-source and checked by many users. If Windows shows a blue or yellow warning, click "More Info" then "Run Anyway." This happens with many legitimate programs that don't yet have a paid digital certificate.

I have a different AMD GPU. Will it still work?

Most likely yes, though speed may vary. The app automatically adjusts to any compatible AMD Radeon card that supports ROCm. No manual configuration is needed.

What file formats can I analyze?

You can copy and paste any text. You can also open EPUB files (most common ebook format) and plain text files (like .txt).

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs 100% locally on your computer. Your documents and notes never leave your machine.
